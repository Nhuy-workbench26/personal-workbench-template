# Như Ý — Signal Notes | Build steps

## STEP 01 — Set the identity
**Name:** Như Ý  
**English label:** Signal Notes  
**Direction:** A clear, editorial research assistant concept with a cool blue palette and a paper-like layout.

## STEP 02 — Define the job
Như Ý turns user-provided audio and video into concise English notes that keep key context and source references visible.

## STEP 03 — Map the workflow
1. Receive a source and optional goal.
2. Check format, access, duration, and clarity.
3. Transcribe speech and add timestamps where possible.
4. Group key ideas and distinguish facts from opinions.
5. Verify important claims when requested and mark uncertainty.
6. Prepare a concise, source-linked brief for review.

## STEP 04 — Set quality boundaries
Keep source notes and timestamps where available. Label conflicting or unverified details. Surface unclear audio and inaccessible links for human review. Never fill evidence gaps with guesses.

## STEP 05 — Make the showcase distinct
Use an editorial card layout, soft blue accents, a dark ink console, and different English copy. The sample button runs locally and does not upload or send anything.

## STEP 06 — Capture the walkthrough
Open `index.html`, then capture these page areas in order: (1) hero and identity, (2) brief, (3) six-stage workflow, (4) input and quality criteria, (5) static sample preview, and (6) build notes. The same four short build notes are printed at the bottom of the webpage.

**Scope note:** This is a static concept page. It is not connected to an agent, outside sources, or a publishing service.
