01 / The brief

From long listens
to useful notes.

Như Ý helps organize material you provide. This page is a concept showcase: it does not connect to an agent, external sources, or a publishing channel.

Role / Research assistant

Audio & video summarizer

Collect key ideas from talks, interviews, explainers, and video reports, then present them in a format that is easy to scan and trace back.

One-sentence mission

Keep the context. Make the signal clear.

Given a file or accessible link, Như Ý checks the input, extracts the main ideas, labels uncertainty, and prepares a concise English brief with source references.

02 / Six-stage process

Careful in.
Clear out.

Every summary follows a reviewable path, from the first link to the final handoff.

01Input

Receive the source

Collect a file or public link, plus the topic, audience, and desired length when available.

02Check

Inspect access

Confirm the format, duration, audio clarity, and whether the material can be processed.

03Capture

Transcribe & timestamp

Extract spoken content and add timestamps or source IDs where the input supports them.

04Select

Find the key ideas

Group the central points by theme and separate reported facts from opinions or proposals.

05Verify

Mark what is known

Check important claims against independent sources when requested; flag gaps or conflicts.

06Deliver

Prepare the brief

Write a concise English summary with source notes, timestamps, and clear review markers.

Human review: unclear audio, blocked links, conflicting claims, or missing evidence are surfaced for review. The assistant should state limits instead of filling gaps with guesses.
03 / Input & quality

Traceable by design.

A useful brief makes it easy to see what came from the source and what still needs checking.

Accepted material
  • 01 Audio files such as MP3 or WAV
  • 02 Video files with audible speech
  • 03 Accessible public links and source bundles
Success criteria
  • 01 Main points reflect the source and requested goal
  • 02 Sources and timestamps are included where available
  • 03 Unverified or conflicting details are clearly labeled
